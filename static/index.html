Agent 3

Review Moderation Agent

Detects coordinated fake-review rings by combining a text classifier (88% precision) with account-age and timing pattern rules.

Checks: is a burst of 5+ reviews arriving within an hour? Are 60%+ of reviewers brand-new accounts (under 7 days old)? Does the text classifier's average score suggest fake/AI-written text? Two or more of these triggered together means a coordinated ring.
